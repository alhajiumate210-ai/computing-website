WHO WE ARE

About our department

The Department of Computing encourages students to understand technology and use it creatively, responsibly, and effectively.

Our mission

To support learning and practical problem-solving in computing through a strong foundation in digital technologies, collaboration, and continuous improvement.

Our vision

To help develop confident learners who can adapt to changing technology and contribute positively to their communities.

What students learn

  • Problem-solving and computational thinking
  • Website and software development
  • Computer networks and information systems
  • Teamwork, communication, and project management
